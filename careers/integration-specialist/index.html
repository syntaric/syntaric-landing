Integration specialist, client projects

Remote within Europe, with occasional client travel Full-time, employed or as a contractor €4,500 gross per month

Syntaric is a small healthcare interoperability consultancy. Hospitals, regional care networks and national health programmes hire us to get their systems sharing patient data. We have worked with clients in the Netherlands, the United Kingdom, Iceland, Slovenia and the United States.

In this role you work on client projects. You find out which data has to go where and why, then make it happen with open standards, and often with our own products, openFHIR and Unio.

What you will do

  • Work on client projects from the first workshop to go-live, often as part of the client's own team.
  • Map clinical data between models: openEHR templates, FHIR profiles, and the formats a hospital system actually sends.
  • Connect hospital systems such as Epic, Cerner Millennium and ChipSoft HiX, and the standard ways of exchanging documents and finding patients (IHE XDS, PIX and PDQ).
  • Deploy and configure openFHIR and Unio for clients. Tell the product team what is missing.
  • Write it down: integration specifications, test plans, and documentation the client can take over.
  • Travel to clients now and then, mostly in the Netherlands.

What we are looking for

  • You have connected systems that run in production: APIs, messaging, data mapping, and working out why two systems disagree.
  • You know at least one healthcare standard well (FHIR, HL7 v2, openEHR or IHE profiles). If your integration experience is from another field and you want to learn these, tell us.
  • You are comfortable with code and tooling. You can read and write some Java or another language, query with SQL, and work on the command line and with Docker.
  • You explain things clearly in English to clinicians, IT staff and project managers.
  • You like working directly with clients, sometimes on site.

We judge by the work, not by years of experience.

Good to have

  • Advance knowledge of openEHR, FHIR, IHE, ..
  • Experience with Dutch healthcare infrastructure

What we offer

  • Pay: €4,500 gross per month. For contractors we agree an equivalent rate that makes sense for both of us.
  • Where: remote within Europe, with occasional travel to clients.
  • Varied work: hospitals, regional networks and national programmes in several countries.
  • Work on the standards themselves: we sit on the openEHR Specifications Editorial Committee Experts Panel and co-wrote FHIR Connect and the openEHR Federation specification.

How to apply

Email info@syntaric.com with your CV or LinkedIn profile and a few lines about an integration you have worked on.

  1. A 30-minute call to get to know each other.
  2. A technical conversation about real work: yours and ours.
  3. Meeting the team.

We reply to every application within a week.

Interested? Write to us at info@syntaric.com.
