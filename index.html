Selected work

RSO Zuid-Limburg · 2026

One view of a patient across a region's care providers

In Zuid-Limburg every care provider keeps its own patient records, on purpose. An application that needed a patient's full history had to ask each of them separately.

With RSO Zuid-Limburg and the openEHR standards committee we wrote a specification for federating openEHR repositories, so they answer as one. We built an open reference implementation and our product Unio on it. The specification was approved in October 2026.

Read about the approval

HL7 Converge & Collaborate, Dublin · 2026

Two health data standards working together, built live in a workshop

We ran the hands-on "Let's Build" session. In one afternoon, participants connected a patient record system to an international exchange standard: stored records came out as an international patient summary, and incoming data was stored in the record.

The setup: an openEHR repository, Firely Server and openFHIR, returning a FHIR International Patient Summary.

Read the session write-up

Proof of concept · 2026

Nursing handover between care organisations, on open standards

When a patient moves from a hospital to a nursing home or home care, the nurses' handover has to go with them. In the Netherlands that handover follows a national standard, eOverdracht.

We built a working proof of concept of eOverdracht on a hybrid openEHR and FHIR architecture, using the routing and care-services building blocks of the Dutch Generic Functions programme.

Products based on strong underlying standards

openFHIR for bridging openEHR and FHIR and Unio, an openEHR Federation Gateway

openFHIR translates patient records between the way one system stores them and the way another expects to receive them, in both directions.

It maps between openEHR and FHIR and implements FHIR Connect, an open specification for describing these mappings, which we co-created. It was the bridge between openEHR and FHIR in the "Let's Build" session at HL7 Converge & Collaborate, Dublin 2026, and in our International Patient Summary webinar.

Open source (Apache 2.0), with a commercial Enterprise version.

open-fhir.com

Unio lets an application see a patient's records from every care provider in a region as if they were in one place, while each record stays where it was created.

It makes several openEHR repositories answer as one, and implements the openEHR Federation Tier with AQL specification, version 1.0.0, approved by the openEHR SEC in October 2026.
